Wholesale supply

Factory-direct supply from Türkiye for individual hotels and hotel groups in the quantities required.

Fabrics and construction

Velvet, piqué, terry and VIP fabrics are custom-produced from yarn through weaving and dyeing. A cardboard base holds the shape, with density-13 white foam for cushioning. Low-moisture cardboard uses industrially grown timber and recycled material. Weight varies by model from 380 to 520 g/m².

Soles

The manufacturer describes the standard non-slip sole as phthalate-free with grip on wet and dry surfaces. The Eco finish is applied to cardboard. EVA is soft and odourless.

Packaging options

A nonwoven pouch resists tearing in transit and can also be used to dust shoes. Clear cellophane is the standard pack.

Branding and colours

Embroidery suits double-fold terry and velvet models. Pantone+ Solid Coated dye matching and up to four print colours in one pass are available. The catalogue lists black, royal blue and grey as stock fabrics; green, pink and brown illustrate custom orders.

Production notes

Custom-logo quantities may vary by ±5–10%. Fabric texture can vary slightly between batches. Without a Pantone code, the closest colour to the logo is selected.
